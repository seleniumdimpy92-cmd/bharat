About our Andaman taxi service

Bharat Transport & Tourism is a Port Blair–based, Govt. of India registered tour operator running one of the largest Andaman cab services in the islands. With our own fleet of sedans (Wagon R, Swift Dzire), Innova SUVs, 12-seater & 17-seater Tempo Travellers and 13-seater Tata Wingers, plus partner drivers stationed at Havelock (Swaraj Dweep), Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep) and Diglipur, we cover every inhabited island in the Andamans.

You can book a cab from Port Blair airport (IXZ) starting at just ₹500, take a Havelock half-day sightseeing tour from ₹2,200, or hire a Tempo Traveller for a group day-trip to Baratang Limestone Caves, Mud Volcano, Cellular Jail, Corbyn's Cove, Chidiya Tapu, Radhanagar Beach, Kalapathar, Bharatpur Beach, Laxmanpur Beach or Saddle Peak / Ross & Smith Islands at Diglipur. We also book Makruzz, Green Ocean, Nautika and Govt. ferry tickets for inter-island transfers — one team, one phone number for cabs and cruises.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also book cruise & ferry tickets?

Yes. We book Makruzz, Green Ocean, Nautika and the Govt. ferry. Send us your dates on WhatsApp and we will lock the best class & timing for your itinerary.

Can the same cab be used at Havelock or Neil after the ferry?

Vehicles do not cross the sea — but our network does. We arrange a fresh vehicle waiting at the Havelock or Neil jetty as soon as you arrive, with the same standard of service.

How big a group can your Tempo Traveller take?

We run three group-vehicle sizes — a 12-seater Tempo Traveller, a 13-seater Tata Winger, and a 17-seater Tempo Traveller — all with pushback seats and AC. For larger groups we club two TTs or use a mini-bus; just tell us your headcount.

How do I pay?

You can pay online via Razorpay (UPI / card / netbanking), or in cash to the driver. For multi-day bookings we usually take a small advance to confirm the vehicle.

Is there a cancellation charge?

Cancellations more than 48 hours before pickup are free. See our cancellation policy for full details.

Ready to lock your ride?

Tell us your dates and itinerary — we will send a same-day quote with vehicle options.
