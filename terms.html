Cancellations within the 0–7 day window are also treated as no-show — any partial balance already paid is forfeited as well.

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method.

Price-Lock Fee — Special Rules

As an alternative to paying the full booking advance up-front, customers may choose to lock today’s package price for 10 days by paying a flat fee of ₹500 per traveller. The price-lock is governed by simpler (and stricter) rules than the standard booking advance:

  • The ₹500 / traveller lock fee is strictly non-refundable. It pays for the 10-day price guarantee — that service is delivered the moment payment is received, regardless of whether you go on to book.
  • If you convert the price-lock into a confirmed booking within 10 days, the entire amount you paid (₹500 × number of travellers) is credited toward your booking advance. You only pay the difference at booking time.
  • If you do not book within the 10-day window, the lock automatically expires and the fee is forfeited. The locked price is no longer guaranteed and you may need to pay the prevailing rate at the time of any future booking.
  • You may cancel an active price-lock at any time from your “My Bookings” dashboard, but doing so does not refund the fee. Cancellation only stops the lock from showing up as “active” in your account.
  • Locks are tied to the specific package and headcount at the time of payment. Changing the package, increasing the headcount, or extending the travel duration may require a fresh price-lock.
  • The price-lock does not guarantee availability of hotels, ferries, flights or any third-party inventory — only the package price. If supplier inventory is unavailable when you book, we will offer the closest equivalent at the locked price (or refund the fee at our discretion).

In short: Lock fee is non-refundable. Book within 10 days → the entire fee counts toward your booking advance. Don’t book within 10 days → the fee is lost and the price guarantee ends.

Worked example: A couple pays ₹500 × 2 = ₹1,000 to lock the price of a Standard package on Day 0. On Day 7 they decide to book and pay the booking advance of ₹6,000 × 2 = ₹12,000 — minus the ₹1,000 already paid. Net advance payment = ₹11,000. If instead they had not booked by Day 11, the ₹1,000 lock fee would have been forfeited.
